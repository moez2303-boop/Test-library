--- exercise kanji_reading
? 「後継ぎ」の{読|よ}み{方|かた}は？ || How do you read 後継ぎ?

- こうけいぎ

- ごけいぎ
! 後継ぎ is read あとつぎ: 後 (あと, after) + 継ぐ (つぐ, to succeed). It means the person who takes over a family business.
--- exercise grammar
? どうせ{失敗|しっぱい}する＿＿。 || It's bound to fail anyway.
+ に決まってる
- わけがない
- どころではない
- にすぎない
! 〜に決まっている means "is bound to, is certain to". わけがない would mean the opposite ("can't possibly fail"), which doesn't fit どうせ.

- こそ
- しか
! 〜さえ〜ば means "as long as / if only": 仕事さえあれば = "as long as there is work".
