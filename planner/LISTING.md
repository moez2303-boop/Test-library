## FAQ replies (save as Etsy "saved replies")

**"How do I import into GoodNotes?"** Open the zip in the Files app, tap the
PDF, choose Share → GoodNotes → Import as New Document. Hyperlinks work in
GoodNotes' default *Read-only/Pen* mode, but not while the Lasso tool is selected.

**"Links don't work?"** Make sure you're not in the text/lasso tool, and that
you imported the PDF itself (not a screenshot or an image import).

**"Can I get it undated / a different year?"** Each new year is a free
regeneration (`python3 generate.py --year 2028`). Tell buyers next year's
version will be listed in September.
